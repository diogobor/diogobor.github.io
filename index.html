About

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ), Brazil, and an M.Sc. in Computer Science specializing in Artificial Intelligence from UFRJ's Systems Engineering and Computer Science Program. I also hold a Ph.D. in Biosciences and Biotechnology from the Carlos Chagas Institute / Fiocruz, where I developed SIM-XL, a search tool for complex cross-linked peptide scenarios that supports structural proteomics and protein–protein interaction studies.

During my first postdoc in computational proteomics at Institut Pasteur in Paris, I built new bioinformatics solutions for top-down proteomics data. In my second postdoc at the Leibniz-FMP in Berlin, I focused on cross-linking mass spectrometry data analysis using cleavable reagents and on tools to accelerate peptide identification.

Currently, I'm a Research Scientist in computational proteomics at Abbott Cancer Diagnostics in Munich, Germany.
